Section 27 Evidence Act (now Section 23 BSA)

How much of information received from accused may be proved

Then and now

Section 27 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 covers: how much of information received from accused may be proved. The matching provision today is Section 23 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, Section 23 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Incorporates the rule on proving information received from an accused in custody that distinctly leads to the discovery of a fact as a proviso to Section 23.
  • Substantially unchanged doctrine of custodial discovery enacted as a proviso to Section 23.
