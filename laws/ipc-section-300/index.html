Section 300 IPC (now Section 101 BNS)

Murder

Then and now

Section 300 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 covers: murder. The matching provision today is Section 101 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

  • Indian Penal Code, 1860Section 300Murder
    →
    Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023Section 101Murder

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Substantially unchanged; Exception 1 retains the defense of grave and sudden provocation.
  • Preserved definition of murder and five statutory exceptions under Section 101.
