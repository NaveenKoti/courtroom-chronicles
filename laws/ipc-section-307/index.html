Section 307 IPC (now Section 109 BNS)

Attempt to murder

Then and now

Section 307 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 covers: attempt to murder. The matching provision today is Section 109 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

  • Indian Penal Code, 1860Section 307Attempt to murder
    →
    Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023Section 109Attempt to murder

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Offence of attempting murder reorganized under Section 109 of the new code.
  • Re-enacted comprehensive penal sanctions punishing deliberate physical assaults endangering human life.
