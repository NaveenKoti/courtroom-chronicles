Section 336 IPC (now Section 125 BNS)

Act endangering life or personal safety of others

Then and now

Section 336 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 covers: act endangering life or personal safety of others. The matching provision today is Section 125 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

  • Indian Penal Code, 1860Section 336Act endangering life or personal safety of others
    →
    Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023Section 125Act endangering life or personal safety of others

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Re-enacted with enhanced fines and imprisonment terms for rash or negligent acts endangering human life or personal safety.
  • Consolidated into Section 125 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (covering former Sections 336, 337, and 338) with revised fine and imprisonment limits.
