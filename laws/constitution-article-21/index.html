Article 21 Constitution

Protection of life and personal liberty

Then and now

Article 21 of the Constitution of India covers: protection of life and personal liberty. It has not been replaced; the same provision applies today.

  • Constitution of IndiaArticle 21Protection of life and personal liberty
    →
    Unchanged

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Unchanged.
