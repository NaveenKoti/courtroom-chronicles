Section 45 Evidence Act (now Section 39 BSA)

Opinions of experts

Then and now

Section 45 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 covers: opinions of experts. The matching provision today is Section 39 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, Section 39 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Substantially unchanged; expert scientific opinions on DNA and ballistics are preserved under Section 39.
  • Substantially unchanged; preserves admissibility of biological and forensic expert testimony.
