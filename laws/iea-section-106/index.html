Section 106 Evidence Act (now Section 109 BSA)

Burden of proving fact especially within knowledge

Then and now

Section 106 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 covers: burden of proving fact especially within knowledge. The matching provision today is Section 109 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

  • Indian Evidence Act, 1872Section 106Burden of proving fact especially within knowledge
    →
    Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023Section 109Burden of proving fact especially within knowledge

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Substantially unchanged burden of proof regarding facts specially within personal knowledge.
  • Substantially unchanged; governs burden of proof for facts peculiarly within personal knowledge.
