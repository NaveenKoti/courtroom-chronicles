Section 302 IPC (now Section 103 BNS)

Punishment for murder

Then and now

Section 302 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 covers: punishment for murder. The matching provision today is Section 103 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

  • Indian Penal Code, 1860Section 302Punishment for murder
    →
    Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023Section 103Punishment for murder

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Retains death or imprisonment for life and fine for murder under sub-section (1), and adds a separate sub-section (2) addressing murder committed by a group of five or more persons on specified grounds.
  • Substantially unchanged; restructured under Chapter VI of the new penal code.
