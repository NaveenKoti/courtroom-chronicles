Section 304A IPC (now Section 106 BNS)

Causing death by negligence

Then and now

Section 304A of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 covers: causing death by negligence. The matching provision today is Section 106 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, Section 106(1) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

  • Indian Penal Code, 1860Section 304ACausing death by negligence
    →
    Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023Section 106Causing death by negligence
    Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023Section 106(1)Causing death by negligence

The new criminal codes apply only to offences on or after 1 July 2024. Older cases are still decided under the old law.

What changed

  • Expanded into subsection (1) for rash and negligent acts, with increased penalties for failure to report accidents.
  • Re-enacted under Section 106(1) with enhanced penalties for rash and negligent acts causing death.
